# AI Engineer Dictionary — Master A–Z Vocabulary

> Status: Proposed vocabulary for V1. This is a curated starting scope, not a claim that every term is already written or reviewed.

**Current proposed terms: 343**

## Editorial rules

- Include a term when it is useful to understand, build, evaluate, deploy, secure, or research AI systems.
- Prefer canonical names; put common abbreviations and aliases in the entry's metadata or body rather than creating duplicate pages.
- Difficulty describes the expected background, not the importance of the concept.
- Notebook priority: Essential = hands-on treatment strongly recommended; Recommended = useful practical demonstration; Optional = prose/math/diagram is usually sufficient.
- A term may be reclassified, merged, renamed, or removed during editorial review.

## Review notes

- Similar but distinct ideas should remain separate when they solve different problems (for example, embeddings and vector databases).
- Related aliases should normally be searchable synonyms, not separate entries (for example, RAG and Retrieval-Augmented Generation).
- Some entries may require a scope note to distinguish overloaded terms (for example, attention, memory, agent, inference, and context).
- Keep the vocabulary broad enough to cover AI engineering beyond LLM applications, including classical ML, statistics, robotics, control, computer vision, and infrastructure.
